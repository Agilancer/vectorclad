# Billboard Ad Textures

60 generated billboard artwork PNGs for videogame use.

- 40 eccentric product ads with English copy and Japanese commercial-poster-inspired art direction.
- 20 serious corporate B2B campaign ads.
- Native image dimensions: 1672×940 (4 files) or 1672×941 (56 files), all landscape and close to 16:9.
- manifest.json records each brand, filename, and generation prompt. dimensions.json lists each image's exact dimensions and byte size.
