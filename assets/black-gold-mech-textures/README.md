# Matte black and gold mech armor textures

10 full-page repeating armor surface designs plus 2 plain material textures generated with built-in ImageGen. Matte black armor, warm gold trim, and solid gold reinforcement pieces. Includes broad plating, layered chevrons, longitudinal plates, joint rings, vents, hexagons, decorative inlays, worn armor, dense technical panels, and heavy interlocking plates. The two plain pages contain only matte black or satin gold micrograin, without panel details.

These are continuous material pages rather than sprite atlases. Prompts requested seamless repetition across both axes. Check joins at your intended UV scale before final use: generated edge alignment is not a guarantee of pixel-perfect seamlessness. Original PNGs are preserved without scaling. Requested resolution was 2048 x 2048; native returned dimensions are recorded in manifest.json with exact prompts and hashes.

Use as sRGB base-color maps. Set high roughness for matte black armor; use a metallic mask and suitable roughness for the gold sections. No PBR companion maps are supplied. Some generated bevel shading is present. Engine import has not been tested.
